What OPB Does

Designed for quantitative analysts, options traders, and active market participants seeking systematic decision support rather than speculative guessing.

Sector Flow & Radar

Enterprise Risk Architecture

Hard circuit-breaker limits, portfolio drawdown monitoring, broker margin radars, and automated safety lockouts that prevent rogue trades or margin stress.

How OPB Works — Algorithmic Pipeline

From raw exchange tick data to deterministic signal verification, OPB operates as an automated 5-stage quantitative pipeline.

1 Market Data Ingestion & Tick Normalization

Real-time market feeds stream from authorized exchange endpoints and broker feeds. Ingested ticks are timestamped, validated for monotonicity, checked for anomalies, and mapped to standardized financial instrument models.

2 Feature Engineering & Order Block Analytics

Data streams are transformed into multi-timeframe quantitative features: exponential moving average convergences, volatility regime classifications, VWAP deviations, option implied volatility surfaces, and institutional order clusters.

3 Consensus Signal Engine & Tier Gate

Signals are NOT generated by single indicators. Multiple independent quantitative models must establish consensus (directional trend, volatility confirmation, options flow backing, and risk-reward ratio >= 1:2) before an alert is accepted.

4 Risk-Checked Dispatch & User Notification

Approved signals pass through per-user quota checks, administrative activation verification, and risk thresholds before dispatching via WebSockets to the cockpit and email to registered, approved subscribers.

Governed Execution Modes & Safety Boundaries

Safety and risk containment are hardcoded into the platform's core governance architecture.

Fail-Closed Execution Invariant

OPB is strictly locked in PAPER / SIMULATION mode. The application runs with EXECUTION_MODE=SIGNAL_ONLY, LIVE_TRADING_LOCKOUT=True, and BROKER_AUTO_ROUTING=DISCONNECTED. The platform does not place automated live orders on external exchange accounts without explicit, verified operator override.
