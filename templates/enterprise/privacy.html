Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: October 2026 Version: v2.61 Jurisdiction: India (DPDPA 2023 Compliant)

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy governs how OPB ("Options & Pairs Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, encrypts, and protects personal and technical data when you interact with our quantitative trading cockpit, web applications, and notification endpoints.

We operate on principles of privacy-by-design, data minimization, and tenant isolation. We collect only what is strictly necessary to authenticate your access, deliver quantitative market signals, and maintain platform security.

Email: privacy@opb.app

2. Information We Collect & Cryptographic Controls

We process the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Username, registered email address, and account role. Passwords are NEVER stored in plaintext. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using standard PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and per-user unique cryptographic salts.
  • Session Identifiers: Ephemeral, cryptographically random session tokens stored in secure, HTTPOnly browser cookies with strict expiry.
  • Notification Preferences: Configured email destinations, subscribed trading instrument categories (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, Commodities, Stocks), and delivery thresholds.
  • Security & Audit Telemetry: Client IP addresses, access timestamps, HTTP user-agents, and authentication logs processed exclusively for brute-force mitigation, DDoS defense, and rate-limiting.
What We NEVER Collect or Store:

We NEVER request, collect, or store your bank account passwords, trading terminal master PINs, UPI MPINs, or two-factor OTPs. When broker APIs are configured, tokens are handled strictly within isolated memory and stored under per-tenant encrypted credential stores.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is processed strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Providing secure user authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), and session management.
  • Dispatching algorithmic market signals, options analytics alerts, and daily analytical summaries to authorized and approved subscribers.
  • Preventing credential stuffing, unauthorized API probing, spam submissions, and infrastructure abuse.
  • Monitoring application uptime, container health, and latency performance across the Indian market trading hours.

4. Tenant Isolation & Zero Data Monetization

We DO NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data. Your information is never sold to marketing networks, brokers, advertisement exchanges, or third-party data brokers.

OPB employs strict multi-tenant boundary isolation. User configurations, watchlists, permission tiers, and signal history are logically segregated. No user can view or access another user's private preferences or notification configurations.

5. Cookies & Client-Side Storage

We do not use third-party tracking, advertising, or cross-site profiling cookies. We use only essential cookies and local storage tokens:

  • opb_session Cookie: Strictly essential session cookie with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure flags to maintain your authenticated login state.
  • Browser LocalStorage: Used exclusively to store your preferred visual theme (e.g. dark-cyber, dracula-purple, ivory-gold, midnight-slate, emerald-matrix) and dashboard UI layout preferences. No sensitive personal data is placed in localStorage.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights (DPDPA 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and international data governance frameworks, you maintain the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information stored in your account profile.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct your profile data, email address, and notification preferences at any time via Account Profile.
  • Right to Erasure & Account Deletion: You may request complete erasure of your account, preferences, and authentication records by contacting support@opb.app.
  • Automated Session Purge: Expired session tokens and single-use password reset tokens are automatically purged by automated background cleanup routines every 15 minutes.

7. Security Architecture & Safeguards

OPB implements rigorous defense-in-depth security:

  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) with dynamic nonces, prohibiting unauthorized inline scripts and external CDNs.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) and anti-clickjacking frame restrictions.
  • Universal copy/paste and cut restrictions on password and authentication inputs to mitigate clipboard hijackers and credential sniffing.
  • Automated rate-limiting, honeypot defenses, and anti-abuse safeguards on all public forms and authentication endpoints.
